Ecological Momentary Assessment

So I did a thing …

In 2013, I realized that there was no platform available to conduct ecological momentary assessment (EMA) and experience sampling method (ESM) research. Yes, there were survey platforms available, but none could conveniently do all the following:

  • Schedule surveys
  • Send notifications and reminders
  • Adapt to participant time zones
  • Link surveys anonymously over time
  • Dashboard ongoing response rates
  • Pay participants
  • Visualize incoming data
  • Connect to wearables

And so I created ExpiWell to do all of that — and more!

We now proudly serve more than 5,000 researchers around the world. ExpiWell is continuing to grow to help with ESM and EMA research.
