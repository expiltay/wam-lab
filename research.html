Research

Some highlights

How do we conceptualize well-being and how can we improve the well-being of societies, organizations, and individuals? Our work spans five interconnected areas.

Taxonomies

Developing and testing integrative taxonomies in industrial-organizational psychology and beyond.

Key Publications

Toward a Dimensional Model of Vocational Interests

Key Publications

Establishing Construct Continua in Construct Validation: The Process of Continuum Specification

Ideal Point Modeling of Non-Cognitive Constructs: Review and Recommendations for Research
Tay, L., & Ng, V. (2018). Frontiers in Psychology. doi: 10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02423
